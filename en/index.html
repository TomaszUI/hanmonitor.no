See your electricity use directly from the meter

HAN Monitor is a local HAN reader in development for Norwegian AMS smart electricity meters. The goal is near-real-time values, simple Wi‑Fi access and local data without a mandatory cloud service.

Near real time

Read power, current and voltage when the meter and data profile provide these values.

Local first

The project focuses on browser access in your home network and keeping normal use independent of a required cloud service.

Norwegian AMS/HAN

Kaifa/Nuri works in the current prototype. Aidon and Kamstrup are still being validated in real installations.

Smart home

MQTT and a local API are planned for Home Assistant and other local systems.

Who is this page for?

This English version is intended for international residents, installers and technical users in Norway who prefer English documentation. HAN Monitor is not presented as a universal European smart-meter reader.
